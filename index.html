Compute to wellbeing

A simulation of how growth in AI compute could flow through automation, jobs and incomes to people's life satisfaction, 2026 to 2060.

Built on GATE Compute, automation and output follow Epoch AI's GATE model of AI and the economy. Jobs, incomes and life satisfaction are a layer added on top. An independent project, not affiliated with Epoch AI.

Every yearly variable is a node showing the formula it is computed with, grouped from compute down to wellbeing. Grey arrows read values from the same year. Violet arrows carry a value into next year: they end at the dashed boxes on the right of each group. The engine builds the diagram from the same formulas it runs, so the diagram always matches the model. Hover, focus or tap a node to pick out its arrows; select it to see its distribution over time.

A personal project by Stéphane Thibaud. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Epoch AI. It draws on Epoch's public data and its GATE model, cited in the Method tab. It was built with Claude Code, an AI coding assistant, under his direction; every assumption links to its source so it can be checked. The engine is Haskell compiled to WebAssembly and runs entirely in your browser.
